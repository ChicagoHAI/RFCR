**Precise Enumeration of Items Within a Designated Category**

---

### Guidelines:

1. Determine the specified category in the prompt (Objects, Vegetables, Fruits, Animals, or Musical Instruments).  
2. Count only those items that belong to the designated category.  
3. For every item referenced in the text:  
   - If a specific quantity is mentioned (e.g., "three bananas"), use that exact number.  
   - If no quantity is provided, count the item as one.  
   - Plural forms (e.g., "couches," "garlics") indicate multiple items; count accordingly (e.g., "four couches" = 4, "garlics" = multiple garlic bulbs).  
   - Compound phrases such as "heads of broccoli," "stalks of celery," or "garlics" represent multiple units and should be counted based on the stated or implied quantity.  
4. Exclude any items that do not belong to the requested category.  
5. Count each item individually, even if quantities are not explicitly stated (e.g., "a garlic" = 1, "two garlics" = 2).  
6. Refer to the category lists provided to confirm whether an item belongs in the specified category before counting.

---

### Category Reference:

**Objects**  
Examples: couch, bed, table, lamp, car, chair, toaster, stove, fridge, oven, microwave.

**Vegetables**  
Examples: cabbage, carrot, celery stalk, onion, garlic, cauliflower, broccoli head, lettuce head, potato, yam.  
Notes:  
- Count each head, stalk, or bulb separately.  
- Plural "garlics" means multiple bulbs of garlic.  
- Do not include fruits, animals, or other categories.

**Fruits**  
Examples: apple, banana, orange, peach, plum, nectarine, raspberry, blackberry, strawberry, grape, blueberry.  
Notes:  
- Count each unit or plural form exactly.  
- Exclude vegetables, animals, or other categories.

**Animals**  
Examples: mouse, snail, chicken, cat, pig, snake, fish, goat, bear, dog, cow, frog, donkey, rabbit, duck.  
Notes:  
- Correctly count singular and plural forms.  
- Do not count instruments, fruits, vegetables, or objects.

**Musical Instruments**  
Examples: flute, trombone, accordion, clarinet, drum, piano, violin, trumpet.  
Notes:  
- Count each instrument mentioned.  
- Exclude animals, fruits, vegetables, or objects.

### Correct Counting Examples:

- "three heads of broccoli" = 3 vegetables  
- "five carrots" = 5 vegetables  
- "two apples" = 2 fruits  
- "a dog" = 1 animal  
- "three drums" = 3 instruments  
- "a stalk of celery" = 1 vegetable  
- "four couches" = 4 objects  
- "a garlic" = 1 vegetable  
- "two garlics" = 2 vegetables  

---

### Key Points to Remember:

- Multiply counts properly when quantities or plurals are mentioned.  
- Count each item individually, even if no quantity is given.  
- Always count singular items as one (e.g., "a [item]" = 1).  
- Exclude items outside the requested category.  
- Pay close attention to compound nouns and plural forms to ensure accurate totals.
