Cheat Sheet for Difficult Examples: Key Strategies and Details

---

### 1. **Color Identification and Verification**
- When asked if an item is a certain color (yes/no):
  - Confirm the exact color stated in the question.
  - Do NOT confuse similar or related colors (e.g., mauve ≠ purple, fuchsia ≠ magenta).
  - Example: "Is the teddy bear mauve?" → Check if teddy bear is explicitly mauve or another color.

---

### 2. **Position-Based Questions (Arranged in a Row)**
- **Furthest from / Directly to the left/right of:**
  - Identify the order of items exactly as given.
  - "Furthest from X" means the item at the opposite end of the row from X.
  - "Directly to the left/right of X" means the immediate neighbor on that side.
  - Carefully track the sequence; do not assume circular or wrap-around order.
  - Example: If row is [A, B, C], furthest from A is C; directly to the left of B is A.

- **Counting items to the left/right of a specific item:**
  - Count only items strictly on the specified side.
  - When counting "non-[color]" or "non-[attribute]" items, exclude those matching the color/attribute.
  - Example: "How many non-turquoise items to the left of yellow?" → Count all items left of yellow that are NOT turquoise.

---

### 3. **Counting After Removal**
- When items are removed by category (color or object type):
  - Remove all specified items first.
  - Count remaining items of the requested type or color.
  - Pay attention to plural forms and quantities given.
  - Example: "If I remove all the envelopes, how many pink items remain?" → Remove all envelopes, then count pink items left.

---

### 4. **Color Grouping and Similar Colors**
- Be aware of subtle color differences:
  - Mauve, magenta, fuchsia, purple, pink are distinct.
  - Teal and turquoise are distinct but related; check exact usage.
  - Silver and grey are different; silver is metallic.
- Do NOT confuse colors even if they sound similar.

---

### 5. **Multiple Items of Same Type and Color**
- When multiple items of the same type and color are present:
  - Keep track of exact counts.
  - When removing items, remove all matching the criteria.
  - Example: "Three silver scrunchiephone chargers and two magenta scrunchiephone chargers. Remove all magenta items. How many scrunchiephone chargers remain?" → Only silver remain, count = 3.

---

### 6. **"Neither" Questions (Neither X nor Y)**
- Count items that do NOT have either of the specified colors or attributes.
- Example: "How many items are neither red nor brown?" → Exclude all red and brown items, count the rest.
