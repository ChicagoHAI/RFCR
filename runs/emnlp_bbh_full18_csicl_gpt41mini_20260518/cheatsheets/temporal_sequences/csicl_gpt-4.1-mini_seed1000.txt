Cheat Sheet for Solving Time-Interval Location Questions (Focus on Difficult Examples)

---

### Key Principles

1. **Identify Known Time Blocks:**
   - Use all given witness sightings and activities to mark occupied time intervals.
   - Include wake-up time as the earliest possible start.

2. **Check Location Closure Time:**
   - The event location is only accessible before its closing time.
   - The person cannot be at the location after it closes.

3. **Find Free Time Intervals:**
   - Determine gaps between known activities where the person is unaccounted for.
   - These gaps are potential times they could have visited the location.

4. **Exclude Impossible Intervals:**
   - Times before wake-up.
   - Times overlapping with known sightings.
   - Times after location closing.

5. **Consider Continuous Time Blocks:**
   - The visit must fit entirely within a free time block.
   - Partial overlaps with known sightings are invalid.

---

### Difficult Example Patterns & Tips

#### 1. **Short Gaps Between Long Activities**
   - Example: Andrew at coffee shop (Q7)
     - Busy 8am-1pm, 3pm-6pm, free 1pm-3pm → answer is 1pm-3pm.
   - **Tip:** Look for small gaps between long known activities.

#### 2. **Wake-up Time After Some Activities**
   - Example: Jessica at restaurant (Q18)
     - Woke at 3pm, activities start at 3pm → visit must be after last known activity but before closing.
   - **Tip:** Do not consider times before wake-up.

#### 3. **No Witnesses During Large Time Blocks**
   - Example: Sarah at construction site (Q3)
     - Busy 8am-4pm, free 4pm-7pm, then busy 7pm-8pm → visit must be in free block.
   - **Tip:** Large free blocks between sightings are prime candidates.

#### 4. **Overlapping Activities Near Closing Time**
   - Example: Mark at football field (Q6)
     - Busy 7am-9pm, field closes 10pm → only 9pm-10pm free.
   - **Tip:** Check if visit can happen after last known activity but before closing.

#### 5. **Multiple Short Sightings with Small Gaps**
   - Example: Leslie at movies (Q9)
     - Busy 6am-7am, 12pm-2pm, 2pm-7pm → free 7am-12pm.
   - **Tip:** Combine multiple short sightings to find longer free intervals.

#### 6. **Wake-up Time Close to Closing Time**
   - Example: Nancy at bookstore (Q153)
     - Woke 8am, busy 8am-2pm, bookstore closes 9pm → free 2pm-9pm.
   - **Tip:** If wake-up is late, free time may be only after last known activity.

#### 7. **Confusing Overlaps with Multiple Witnesses**
   - Example: Elizabeth at amusement park (Q14)
     - Busy 8am-9pm, park closes 10pm → only 6am-8am free.
   - **Tip:** Carefully check all sightings and closing times to find earliest free slot.

---

### Step-by-Step Approach for Difficult Cases

1. **List all known time intervals (activities, sightings).**
2. **Mark these intervals on a timeline from wake-up to location closing.**
3. **Identify all gaps (free intervals) between these known intervals.**
4. **Exclude any intervals before wake-up or after closing.**
5. **Select the free interval(s) that fit entirely within the location’s open hours.**
6. **Match the free interval(s) to the given options.**

---

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- Choosing intervals overlapping with known sightings.
- Ignoring wake-up time (cannot be before wake-up).
- Ignoring location closing time.
- Selecting partial overlaps instead of full free intervals.
- Confusing the person’s activities with others’ activities.

Question: Today, Jennifer went to the basketball court. Between what times could they have gone?
We know that:
Jennifer woke up at 6am.
Thomas saw Jennifer buying a bike at the bike shop from 6am to 9am.
Samantha saw Jennifer driving to the water park from 9am to 10am.
Steven saw Jennifer fixing their computer at the electronic store from 10am to 1pm.
Mark saw Jennifer stretching at a yoga studio from 3pm to 5pm.
The basketball court was closed after 5pm.
Between what times could Jennifer have gone to the basketball court?
Options:
(A) 9am to 10am
(B) 6am to 9am
(C) 3pm to 5pm
(D) 1pm to 3pm
Answer: (D)
