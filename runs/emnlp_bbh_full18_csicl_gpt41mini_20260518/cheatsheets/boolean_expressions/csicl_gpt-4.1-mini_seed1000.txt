**Cheat Sheet for Evaluating Boolean Expressions with `not`, `and`, `or`**

---

### 1. **Operator Precedence and Evaluation Order**

- **Brackets `( )`**: Always evaluate expressions inside brackets first.
- **Operator precedence (highest to lowest):**  
  `not` > `and` > `or`
- **Associativity:**  
  - `not` is unary, applies right to left (inside out).  
  - `and` and `or` are left-associative (evaluate left to right).

---

### 2. **Handling Multiple `not`s in a Row**

- Count the number of consecutive `not`s applied to a value:
  - **Even number of `not`s**: cancels out, value remains the same.  
    Example: `not not True` → `True`
  - **Odd number of `not`s**: flips the value.  
    Example: `not not not False` → `not (not (not False))` → flips once → `True`
- When nested or chained, evaluate from innermost `not` outward.

---

### 3. **Parsing Ambiguous Expressions with Multiple `not`s and `and`**

- Example: `False or not not not False and True`
  - Evaluate `not`s first, **one by one** from innermost to outermost:
    - `not False = True`
    - `not not False = not (True) = False`
    - `not not not False = not (False) = True`
  - Then evaluate `and`:
    - `True and True = True`
  - Then evaluate `or`:
    - `False or True = True`
- **Key:** Do NOT group `not`s with `and` prematurely; apply `not`s fully before `and`.

---

### 4. **Complex Nested `not`s with Brackets**

- When `not` applies to an expression in brackets, evaluate the bracket first, then apply `not`.
- Example: `not not not not ( not False )`
  - Evaluate inside bracket: `not False = True`
  - Apply `not`s one by one:
    - 1st `not True = False`
    - 2nd `not False = True`
    - 3rd `not True = False`
    - 4th `not False = True`
  - Result: `True`

---

### 5. **Expressions with Multiple `and` and `or`**

- Evaluate all `not`s first.
- Evaluate all `and`s from left to right.
- Evaluate all `or`s from left to right.
- Example: `True and not True or True and True`
  - `not True = False`
  - Evaluate `and`s:
    - `True and False = False`
    - `True and True = True`
  - Expression becomes: `False or True`
  - Evaluate `or`: `False or True = True`

---

### 6. **When `not` Applies to Compound Expressions**

- `not (A or B)` means evaluate `A or B` first, then negate.
- `not (A and B)` means evaluate `A and B` first, then negate.
- Example: `not (True or False) or True`
  - `True or False = True`
  - `not True = False`
  - `False or True = True`

---

### 7. **Common Pitfalls**

- **Do not shortcut `not` evaluation:** Always fully evaluate nested `not`s stepwise.
- **Do not mix `not` with `and` or `or` without respecting precedence:** `not` applies before `and` and `or`.
- **Beware of multiple `not`s without brackets:** Evaluate from innermost to outermost.
- **When in doubt, rewrite expression with explicit parentheses to clarify order.**

---

### 8. **Step-by-Step Approach**

1. **Identify and evaluate all bracketed expressions first.**
2. **Count and evaluate consecutive `not`s carefully, from innermost to outermost.**
3. **Evaluate all `and` operations from left to right.**
4. **Evaluate all `or` operations from left to right.**
5. **Substitute intermediate results back into the expression stepwise.**

---

### 9. **Example Walkthrough**

Expression: `False or not not not False and True`

- Step 1: Evaluate `not`s on `False`:
  - `not False = True`
  - `not not False = not (True) = False`
  - `not not not False = not (False) = True`
- Step 2: Evaluate `and`:
  - `True and True = True`
- Step 3: Evaluate `or`:
  - `False or True = True`
